# Darker, better, faster, stronger!

_Published 2025-04-30 — [Original post](https://toolhouse.ai/blog/darker-better-faster-stronger)_

Darker, better, faster, stronger! We switched to Dark Mode, and we made Agent Studio 5x faster. We are also teasing some roadmap updates. We released dark-mode on the Toolhouse platform. Apr 30, 2025 2 minutes Daniele Bernardi Toolhouse goes dark (mode)! As part of our ongoing mission to provide the best developer experience, we're introducing a sleek new look and feel, powered by Dark Mode.

We've listened to your feedback and are switching to a darker color scheme, a staple for engineers who master the dark arts. Agent Studio just got a whole lot faster But that's not all. We've made significant under-the-hood improvements to Agent Studio, making it up to 5x faster than before! We made a series of improvements to our request pipeline, which brought us an immediate performance boost that we're passing down onto you.

Your agents will also bootstrap faster, and you'll see results more quickly than ever before. Don't believe it? Just try it yourself ! We're also rearchitected our Agent Runs backend to improve the speed of execution of async jobs. Any agents you run asynchronously , including scheduled agents , will now take a shorter time to complete. The best is yet to come These updates are just the beginning.

With tens of thousands of agent runs per month, we won't stop delivering the best runtime-as-a-service for your agents. Over the coming weeks, you can expect to see major updates along with some enhancements.
