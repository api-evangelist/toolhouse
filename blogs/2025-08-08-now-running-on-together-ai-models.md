# Now Running on Together.ai Models!

_Published 2025-08-08 — [Original post](https://toolhouse.ai/blog/now-running-on-together-ai-models)_

Now Running on Together.ai Models! Aug 8, 2025 4 minutes Pete Thambundit We're thrilled to announce that your Toolhouse agents can now run on Together.ai models ! This means you get access to powerful open-source models that you can customize, optimize, and scale according to your exact needs. Why This Changes Everything for Your Agents Advanced Reasoning Capabilities Need your agent to tackle complex problems?

Together.ai offers open weight models such as Qwen and Kimi, with enhanced reasoning abilities at a competitive cost per million token. Your Keys, Your Control Use your own API keys from your preferred service. This means better cost control, usage tracking, and more importantly, privacy. When you use your Together AI keys, your agents will run on the Together inference platform.

Build for real life use cases Picture this: You're managing a development team and need an agent that can assist you with code related task. You don’t want to use the usual big inference providers? Simple, switch to Qwen Coder or Kimi, both hosted by Together.ai . In Toolhouse, simply define what your agent should do. For example, you can say: Build an agent that connects to my Github and reviews a PR I’ll submit You can then configure your agent to Together.ai , while still leveraging Toolhouse’s MCP integration with Github.

Getting Started is Simple You can specify your preferred model right in your agent configuration, and Toolhouse handles all the complex integration work behind the scenes. No need to worry about API management, model hosting, or infrastructure scaling. Ready to Supercharge Your Agents? This integration opens up a world of possibilities for your agents. Whether you're building customer service bots, research assistants, content creators, or specialized workflow automation, you now have the flexibility to choose the perfect model for each task.

Your agents just got a major upgrade. Time to see what they can really do! Ready to get started? Join Toolhouse today and give your agents the superpowers they deserve.
