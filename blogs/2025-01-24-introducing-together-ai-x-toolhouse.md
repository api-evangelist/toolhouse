Let’s dive into more details! Introduction to Together AI Together AI makes it easy to run or fine-tune leading open source models with only a few lines of code. By prioritizing transparency and accessibility, Together AI empowers AI enthusiasts to leverage its open-source models and infrastructure. These tools cater to diverse applications, from text generation to advanced decision-making, ensuring that the result is both powerful and accessible to all.

Learn more about Together AI’s offerings in their official documentation . Introduction to Toolhouse Toolhouse takes function calling and tool use in AI a step further by dynamically equipping AI agents with specialized tools that free AI models of their usual restrictions. Unlike static tool integrations seen in systems like ChatGPT or Cursor IDE Sonnet, Toolhouse plugs into any LLM and adapts to the specific AI model and task at hand.

It dynamically provides precise instructions for tools like web browsing, API access like Google Trends, GitHub Files, Notion and more, or image generation, ensuring seamless execution. This adaptability enables developers to build powerful workflows with minimal effort, reducing complex integrations to just three lines of code.
