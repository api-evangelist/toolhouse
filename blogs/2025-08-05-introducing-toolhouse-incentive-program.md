# Introducing Toolhouse Creator Rewards: Build. Share. Earn.

_Published 2025-08-05 — [Original post](https://toolhouse.ai/blog/introducing-toolhouse-incentive-program)_

Introducing Toolhouse Creator Rewards: Build. Share. Earn. Are you ready to turn your creativity into real rewards? We at Toolhouse are thrilled to announce the launch of our Incentive Program: Toolhouse Creator Rewards - Build. Share. Earn. Aug 5, 2025 6 minutes Homan Mirgolbabaee Are you ready to turn your creativity into real rewards? We at Toolhouse are thrilled to announce the launch of our Incentive Program: Toolhouse Creator Rewards - Build.

Share. Earn. What is the Creator Rewards Program? We want to celebrate and reward the amazing things you build with Toolhouse. Whether you’re a developer or a vibe coder, this is your chance to showcase your Toolhouse-powered solutions, inspire others, and earn cash and exclusive perks along the way. How It Works Build something awesome with Toolhouse. Create a 1-3 minute video demo showing off your solution.

Make sure to: Give your project a clear title and description Explain how you used Toolhouse to build it Walk us through your solution in action Tag Toolhouse in your post Post your video on your favorite social platform (TikTok, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, and more!) Submit your video link through our tracking form Earn rewards as your video gains traction!

There’s no limit to your creativity—submit as many unique demos as you like (up to 3 at a time). Just make sure each one is a different use case. Rewards & Bonuses $10 for every qualifying video that reaches 1,000 views OR 30+ likes OR 10+ shares $10 for each additional platform where your video hits the same milestone $10 bonus for videos with 5,000 views OR 50+ non-author comments OR 50+ shares Limited edition MVP T-shirt for submitting 3 qualifying demos within 3 months Founder Interview : Top creators will be invited for an exclusive chat with Toolhouse founders Rewards are tracked monthly and paid out within 30 days of meeting the requirements.

Example: How You Can Win Imagine you build a "Smart Meeting Summarizer" agent with Toolhouse that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes your Zoom calls. You film a quick demo showing how you set it up, how it works, and how it saves you hours every week. You post it on TikTok and LinkedIn, tag Toolhouse, and submit your links. Your TikTok video gets 1,200 views and 35 likes, and your LinkedIn post gets 40 likes.

That’s $20 in rewards—plus, if you submit two more unique demos, you’ll earn the MVP T-shirt! Community Projects We Absolutely Loved This Week Here are two standout projects built with Toolhouse that show just how creative and powerful agent-based workflows can be: 🎬 Scriptify Studio by Eileen Elieen built "Scriptify" using Toolhouse to create emotionally intelligent conversations between fictional characters.Writers can test out scenes where characters act independently, respond with emotional nuance, and even escalate drama using a "conflict intensity" dial.

It's a whole fresh way for screenwriters and storytellers who want to stress-test their ideas with dynamic, improv-style agents more creatively than ever. 💭 IDK Me by Victoria from Lovable Shipped Built during Lovable’s Shipped program , IDK Me is a bilingual reflection companion built by Victoria to help people slow down, feel, and reconnect. It’s a soft, open space, free for anyone who needs it—where users can express themselves through guided prompts powered by Toolhouse.

What started as a personal project now supports people, creating space for emotional clarity, compassion, and connection.
