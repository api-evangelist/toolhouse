We made Toolhouse VIP with the intention to reward the passionate users who advocate for Toolhouse among their friends or at work. More signups == more perks along the way! In addition to Toolhouse executions, you can also claim swag rewards. We made it real nice, and you can earn up to a nice set of AirPods Max! If you prefer, you can choose to get a USD gift card instead. Simply hit the referral tier and we’ll send you instructions to claim your reward.
