To our Early Access users, thank you for being part of the Toolhouse Community! We sincerely hope you are enjoying connecting AI to the real world and overcoming the usual restrictions that come with building, running, and managing function calling. If you are a waitlisted user, the big day is coming very soon – be ready to experience the best way to build, run, and manage function calling.

Before opening Toolhouse to the general public, we will make some improvements to our internal services. Over the next coming days, we will migrate our authentication system, which require an action on your behalf. What this means for you? We take security and privacy really seriously at Toolhouse, so we cannot migrate your existing password to the new system. To ensure a smooth transition, we'll invalidate your current password on November 18, 2024 .

This means that you’ll need to reset your password when you log in to Toolhouse . What happens to your apps? Your applications will be unaffected by this transition . Your API keys will remain active, and your existing Tools and Bundles will continue to work as expected. However, you will need to reset your password and log in to your account to manage Tools, Bundles, and Execution logs.

What can you do? At the moment, sit back, relax and continue using Toolhouse as you like. We’ll send an email ahead of time to make sure you are all set.
